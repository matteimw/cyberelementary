FAQ

Common questions

What grades is the Cyber Elementary School curriculum for?

Grades 3 through 6. There is a lesson plan book for each grade, plus a complete edition that covers all four grades in one volume.

What topics does it cover?

36 lessons in four units of nine: Cyber Security Basics (passwords, updates, phishing, malware, Wi-Fi, personal data, two-factor authentication, backups, device security), Online Safety (sharing, digital footprints, cyberbullying, social media, online strangers, trustworthy websites, screen time, gaming, reporting), Digital Citizenship & Communication (kindness online, copyright, messaging etiquette, webcam safety, cookies and trackers, public vs. private information, scams, online shopping, footprint cleanup) and AI Security & AI Safety (what AI is, how chatbots work, AI-generated content, bias, privacy with voice assistants, AI and homework, misinformation, protecting data from AI, responsible AI use).

How long is each lesson?

Each lesson is designed for 30–40 minutes.

What does each lesson include?

Learning objectives, a warm-up hook, teacher talking points, a guided skill activity, an independent-practice worksheet, a closure, a quiz with answer key (question count by grade — grade 3: 4, grade 4: 5, grade 5: 5, grade 6: 6) and teacher reflection notes.

Is there a video for each lesson?

Yes. Every one of the 36 lessons has a free companion video on the Cyber Elementary YouTube channel, plus a compilation video for each unit.

Can homeschool families use it?

Yes. The grade-level books are written for classroom teachers and homeschool parents, and the complete edition is suited to homeschool co-ops teaching several grades at once.

Who wrote it?

Mark W. Mattei, a cybersecurity professional with 25+ years of experience. It is published by Baldwin Terney Press LLC.
